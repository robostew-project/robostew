# RoboStew Community Conduct

RoboStew contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation safe, constructive, and technically honest.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect regardless of background, identity, experience, employer, or viewpoint.
- Critique ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- State uncertainty and limitations plainly.
- Respect privacy and never pressure someone to expose credentials or private infrastructure.
- Keep safety concerns visible, especially around physical systems and automation.
- Accept correction and repair mistakes promptly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberately misleading claims about validation, safety, compatibility, or project affiliation.
- Using project spaces to promote harmful or unauthorized physical control.
- Sustained disruption of technical discussion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or participation that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Do not include sensitive personal information in a public report. Contact the maintainers privately at `security@robostew.org` when a conduct report contains security or privacy-sensitive material.
